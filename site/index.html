GoldenV2

A V2Ray/Xray proxy client for Android.

GoldenV2 connects to proxy servers that you configure or subscribe to yourself. It does not operate those servers, and it does not require an account.

Data

Browsing traffic and VPN destination addresses never leave your device except to the proxy server you chose. Server credentials and subscriptions are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Analytics and advertising data is sent only with your consent. See the Privacy Policy for the full description of what is collected and by whom.
